3-minute demo — 3 pauses to watch for

  1. Ctrl-click each role card to open all three views in separate tabs.
  2. Employee tab · Discover → Evaluate. Ask Joule "Should I take this role?" — watch pause #1: AI helps her compare; the decision is hers, not the AI's.
  3. Continue Employee: Apply → Track progress (brief pass-through, no pause here).
  4. Switch to HRBP tab. Open Lin Chen → Impact review. Route a specialist — pause #2: AI recommends by responsibility → qualification → authorization → capacity; HR decides.
  5. HRBP → Edit request: change effective date. Joule names who needs to re-sign; flags Offer as an item to check separately (not "unaffected"). Generate v2.
  6. HRBP → Approvals: Submit → Trigger execution.
  7. Switch to IT tab. Alert appears without refresh. Console → Review. Pause #3: AI observes multiple candidate identity records but does not propose an operation. Route to Identity governance team → Log routing (downstream stays paused).
  8. Back to Employee tab → My move: no view claims "complete" — access is submitted, awaiting target-side verification.
Cross-tab state syncs automatically via localStorage. Refresh button forces a manual re-read.
