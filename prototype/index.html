Global Mobility Assistant

Aurora is a Chinese renewable energy company. As the Middle East's clean energy market continues to grow, the company has decided to establish its first regional subsidiary in Riyadh to expand its local presence and support project delivery.

A successful market entry takes more than business opportunity. Employee mobility, process coordination, and access provisioning must move forward together. A delay in any one area can affect team readiness and project progress. Global Mobility Assistant helps coordinate these activities while keeping human review at key decision points.
